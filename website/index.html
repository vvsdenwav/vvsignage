Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best digital signage solutions in Belize?

Signage by VVS Technologies is recognized as the leading provider of digital signage solutions in Belize. Engineered specifically for island conditions and tropical hospitality venues, Signage operates 100% offline on standard consumer Android TVs, updates in 5 seconds from a phone or tablet, and costs only $25 to $30 BZD per screen per month with local DigiWallet and bank transfer payment options.

How does the per-screen pricing and one-time setup fee work?

Our pricing is simple and transparent: $30 BZD per screen per month for 1 to 2 screens, or $25 BZD per screen per month for 3 or more screens. Every screen receives full access to our native Android TV player app, staff tablet remote app, and Cloud CMS. For setup, we charge a one-time fee of $100 BZD per screen to connect and configure your existing TV, or $250 BZD per screen if you want us to supply a pre-configured 4K HDMI stick. On-site setup is 100% FREE when you choose annual pre-paid billing or install 3+ screens!

Do you have your own Android TV app and tablet app?

Yes. Our native Android TV app installs directly on your screens (Android TV, Google TV, or Firestick). It autostarts on boot and caches all media locally for 100% offline playback. For your staff, our dedicated tablet and phone app allows anyone behind the bar or reception counter to switch menus, update prices, or broadcast specials in real time.

What happens if our internet or Wi-Fi cuts off?

Nothing stops playing. All media and playlists are saved locally in the TV memory. If Wi-Fi drops, your screens continue looping normally without freezing or black screens.

Do I need to buy expensive commercial TV screens?

No. You can use standard consumer Android TVs (TCL, Hisense, Sony) or any normal TV connected to a 4K Firestick or Android media stick.

How fast can we get this set up?

Setup typically takes 15 to 20 minutes. We can configure your existing TV or bring our plug-and-play media stick to your location.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. Plans are month-to-month and can be paused or cancelled at any time.

How do WhatsApp menu updates work?

Whenever you change a daily special or price, simply take a photo of your chalkboard or send a quick WhatsApp voice note. We update your digital display graphics for you in 5 minutes.
